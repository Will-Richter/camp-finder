## Description
Tell Camp Finder where you're headed and it plans a free or cheap camp for every night of the trip, picked for your rig, whether that's a swag, a camper trailer or a 22-foot van.

EVERY NIGHT SORTED
• Over 10,000 camps, rest areas and caravan parks across Australia, built in and working without signal
• Camps chosen for your rig, budget and what you need: toilets, water, showers, dogs, power, campfires
• Want a real bed on night four? Tick "Need a refresh" and it finds a motel or cabin
• Stay an extra night anywhere with one tap

KNOW YOU'LL MAKE IT
• Pick your car and it knows your fuel use, tank size and the extra it burns towing
• Warns you if any stretch is longer than your range, and how many jerry cans to carry
• "Last fuel for 280 km" alerts on the road
• Sunset at camp and the time to leave so you arrive before dark
• Bushfire warnings, heavy rain, extreme heat, steep climbs and low clearances for your rig's height

SEE MORE ON THE WAY
• The sights worth the detour, from Coral Bay to Carnarvon Gorge and the Big Banana
• Tick the ones you want and the trip routes you past them
• Ready-made trips: the Big Lap, the Gibb, the Savannah Way and more

FAMILY CAN FOLLOW ALONG
• Turn on Family sharing and send a code
• Family watch your car and van move on the map, how fast you're going, tonight's camp and where you've camped
• Pause or turn it off any time

KEEP EVERY KILOMETRE
• Start a journey and your route is recorded in the background
• Notes and photos in your travel journal
• Badges for kilometres, states and famous roads like the Nullarbor and the Gibb
• A short video of your trip to share

Camp details come from OpenStreetMap, Australian government open data and other travellers. Camps close and rules change, so always check the signs and local rules when you arrive.

## App Review notes
Camp Finder works fully without an account: plan a trip (e.g. "Bundaberg" to "Cairns"), explore camps on the map, start a journey. An account (email code, no password) is only needed to add camps, rate them, upload photos and share your location with family.
Background location: used only while the user has started a journey (to record the route and give "last fuel" alerts) or has turned on Family sharing (so family they invited can see where they are). Both are started by the user and can be stopped at any time; the blue location indicator is shown.
Microphone/speech: voice search when the user taps the microphone.
Contact: hello@gonelappin.au
