Help and support

Stuck, found a wrong camp, or have an idea? We read every message.

Contact us

Email {contact}. Tell us your phone model and what you were doing when it went wrong, and we'll usually reply within a day or two.

A camp is wrong or closed

Open the camp in the app and tap Closed, Full or Price changed under "Been here lately?". Other travellers see your report straight away. You can also email us the camp name.

Voice search doesn't hear me

In your iPhone's Settings, open Camp Finder and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. With no phone signal, voice search also needs Dictation turned on (Settings, General, Keyboard).

My journey stopped recording

Journeys record in the background. In Settings, Camp Finder, Location, choose Always or While Using the App with Precise Location on, and don't force-close the app while you're driving.

The sign-in email didn't arrive

Check your junk folder for an email from Camp Finder (hello@gonelappin.au). The link works once and expires after an hour, so request a new one if it's old. You can also type the 6-digit code from the email into the app.

Delete your account

In the app, go to My stuff, then Saved, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you've shared. Or email us from the address you signed in with and we'll do it for you.
