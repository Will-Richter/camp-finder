Plan where you'll sleep

Type where you're going, or just say it. You get a free or cheap camp for every night, picked for your rig, whether that's a swag, a camper trailer or a 22-foot van. Want a motel on night four? Tick "Need a refresh" and it finds one.

Each day shows the sunset at camp and the time to leave so you pull in before dark, when the roos come out.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Finding camps, planning trips and recording journeys are free. You don't need an account unless you want to add camps, rate them or share your trip live.

Where does the camp information come from?

From OpenStreetMap, Australian government open data and other travellers, updated every week. Camps close and rules change, so always check the signs and local rules when you arrive.

Does it work without phone signal?

Yes. The camp list is stored on your phone, and you can download a trip's maps and camps before you go. Live fuel prices, photos and weather need signal.

What happens to my location?

Your location stays on your phone. Journeys are recorded on your phone too, and only leave it if you turn on live sharing for your family. Read the privacy policy.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone first, with Android to follow.
